# User Guide

What’s Kano Up To? (Kano Navi) is an unofficial fan board for Kano Mahoro
(鹿乃まほろ). It gathers her public updates in one place: what airs next, the
week's schedule, recent posts and videos, her discography, and her journey
since 2010. Visit
it at [kano.yexca.net](https://kano.yexca.net/).

These pages describe what you can see and do on the site.

- [Now](now.md): the next stream, the profile card, and the links section.
- [Schedule](schedule.md): the weekly guide, the month view, and calendar
  subscriptions.
- [Posts](posts.md): recent posts from both X accounts, the agency's posts
  about Kano, reposts picked for the wall, and the post archive.
- [Videos](videos.md): streams, works, Shorts, and the video archive.
- [Journey](journey.md): milestones since 2010 and the visual archive.
- [Works](works.md): her own releases, the works she is featured on, and her
  video uploads.
- [LINE BLOG archive](lineblog.md): her 2015–2019 blog articles, restored
  with their photos and videos.

## Pages and Settings

Every page shares one header with the site name (with an "Unofficial" mark on
wide screens), the page links (Now, Journey, Works, LINE BLOG, About), a live
Japan-time clock, and language and theme controls. The footer says the site
is fan-made and unaffiliated and links to About and Back to top. **Notes**
(**Rights and notes** on the LINE BLOG archive) opens the page's notes below
it.

- **Languages:** English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. The language
  menu sets the interface language and, separately, the content language:
  the language of video and schedule titles, posts, and LINE BLOG
  translations. Content follows the interface until you pick another
  language; Japanese content shows Japanese wording as written and other
  wording, such as a Chinese bilibili post, translated. Both choices are
  remembered on this device.
- **Themes:** light and dark, following your system until you pick one.
- **Layouts:** every page works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
- **Mainland China server site:** when one is available, visitors who appear
  to be in mainland China see a notice offering it once per visit, and the
  footer keeps a link to it while the interface is in Simplified Chinese. The
  site is deployed independently by its provider.

The About page introduces the project and its author, then lists the apps
and sites that show the board: 小鹿包们的宝物, a mobile app for Android, iOS
and HarmonyOS by the provider of the mainland China server site (with a link
to its download page); Kano Navi for iOS, an iPhone app for iOS 26 and later
that is still in development; and the mainland China server site (with a link
to it). It credits the developers who have contributed code (with a link to
the full contributor list), the sources of the LINE BLOG archive, the help
behind the Works page, and the provider of the mainland China server site,
lists the AI models and technologies that built the site, and states the
license.

## Freshness and Sources

The board shows what it last collected from X, YouTube and bilibili; it is not
a live feed. The profile card's platform icons show, on hover, when each of
X, YouTube, bilibili and the official shop was last read successfully. A
platform not read successfully for a day is marked out of date: its icon
turns amber, and the small dot in the header turns from green to amber; the
dot turns red when the board cannot reach the site. An open board picks up a
newly stored snapshot by itself within a few seconds, without a reload.
When a platform is unavailable, the board keeps showing what it already
knows. Returning to the board shows what this device last saw at once, then
updates it in place.

Schedule entries are labelled **Auto-detected** when they were read from a
post or a schedule image automatically, and **Confirmed** when the site's
maintainer checked them. Every post, video, and event links back to its
original page, and official announcements always take precedence over this
board.
