# Posts

**Posts on X** shows the last three days of posts from both of Kano's
accounts, and always at least the eight newest, however old. Posts are laid
out in whole rows; a long post shows its first lines and **Read more** opens
it in full.

- Filter by account to see one account at a time.
- Open images in a viewer and step through them.
- See like, repost, and reply counts as they were last collected.
- A post that quotes another one shows the quoted post as a nested card that
  links to it on X.

## Past Posts

**Past posts** browses every post the board has stored, month by month, with
the same account filter and per-account counts.
