# Schedule

## Weekly Guide

The weekly schedule is a Monday-first program guide: seven day columns on
wide screens and a day list on phones. Each entry shows its Japan time (or
"time TBA" when only the date is known), its type, its status (upcoming, on
air, ended, TBA, or cancelled), and whether it is Auto-detected or Confirmed.
An entry the maintainer has not confirmed may carry a note when it looks
cancelled.

Use the arrows to move between weeks, **Today** to return to the current
week, or the shortcut to the nearest week that has events. When Kano posts a
weekly schedule image on X, it appears beside the guide and opens in full
with a link to the original post.

## Month View

**Month view** opens a month grid with compact entries (dots on phones).
Move between months, pick a date to list its entries, and show that date's
week on the board.

## Calendar Subscription

**Subscribe** adds the schedule to a calendar app, or copies the
subscription link. The calendar covers the past month and the next three
months in Japan time, refreshes about every hour, and follows the language
you picked on the site. Timed entries appear in your calendar's own time
zone, and entries without a time stay single all-day entries on their Japan
date. Cancelled entries are marked as cancelled, and uncertain ones as
tentative.

Feed readers can also follow recent videos and reservations through the
board's video feed, which pages advertise to browsers and readers.
