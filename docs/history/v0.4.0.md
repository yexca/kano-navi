> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Upgrade notes.** Back up `./data` before pulling the new image.
>
> - **Timed synchronization stops until you turn it back on.** Saved
>   workflows and their timers are replaced by one timer per source
>   (ADR-0003), and the new timers start off. After the upgrade, open `/admin`
>   → Sources and enable the X, YouTube and Bilibili timers you want. Nothing
>   is migrated from the saved workflows.
> - **Incompatible admin API change.** `/api/admin/workflows` is removed,
>   `POST /api/admin/runs` answers `400 invalid_input` to a `workflowId`, runs
>   no longer carry `workflowName`, and the overview's `workflows` becomes
>   `timers`. Use `PUT /api/admin/sources/{source}/schedule`,
>   `POST /api/admin/sources/{source}/run` and one-off step runs instead.
> - The first start adds the `source_schedules` table and the
>   `failure_count`, `failure_fingerprint` and `next_attempt_at` columns of
>   `post_llm_states`. The `workflows` table and older runs stay in the
>   database. Rolling back to `0.3.1` keeps them readable, and its saved
>   workflows run again as they were; restore the backup if anything looks
>   wrong.

> [!NOTE]
> New optional setting: `PUBLIC_ORIGIN`, the site's `http` or `https` origin,
> which makes link previews absolute and gives the Atom feed its site and
> self links. `X_BOOTSTRAP_DAYS` now applies only to an account's first run.
> See [Configuration](../operations/configuration.md). Upgrade with
> `docker compose up -d`; update `KANO_IMAGE` first if it is pinned.

Kano Navi v0.4.0 runs each source on its own timer instead of saved
workflows, reads X and YouTube by count without skipping posts or uploads,
and adds calendar and Atom feeds in the reader's language, link previews,
smaller board images, YouTube's own localized titles and an installable
home-screen app.

<details>
<summary><strong>Public pages and feeds</strong></summary>

- The schedule has a Subscribe menu: `GET /api/calendar.ics` is an
  iCalendar subscription of the events 30 days before to 90 days after
  today (Japan dates, at most 500, coming events first), with all-day
  entries for undated events and `CANCELLED` only for a definitive
  cancellation. A timed event without an end lasts an hour when it is a
  stream and half an hour otherwise, and `LAST-MODIFIED` and `SEQUENCE`
  follow its update time so calendar apps pick up a rescheduled entry.
  `GET /api/feed.xml` is an Atom feed of the latest 50 YouTube videos and
  reservations. Both are built from the stored snapshot, cached for 10
  minutes and answer conditional requests. Both take `?lang=en|ja|zh-CN`
  (English when missing or unknown): titles use the board's translations and
  the fixed wording is localized. The Subscribe menu and the feed discovery
  links follow the reader's language.
- Shared links preview with Open Graph and Twitter card tags; `/history` and
  `/about` have their own title, description and URL. `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` makes
  the preview URLs absolute.
- Paths that are not pages of the site show a not-found view and answer 404
  instead of the board with 200. `robots.txt` asks crawlers to skip
  `/admin`, `/api/` and `/mcp` while leaving both feeds fetchable for
  subscriptions.
- The board can be added to a phone's home screen: a web app manifest
  (standalone, starting at `/`) with 192 and 512 px icons, a maskable icon,
  an `apple-touch-icon` and a PNG favicon. There is no service worker; the
  installed app still reads the live API.
- Board images load smaller copies: `/media/<id>?v=…&w=<width>` (320, 640,
  960, 1280 or 1920) serves a JPEG or PNG resized once and kept in
  `data/cache/variants/`, or the original when it cannot shrink. Originals
  are unchanged and the full-image viewer still opens them. The media step
  stores each downloaded image's width and height and measures up to 50
  older files per run.
- A video with a merged Bilibili upload shows both platform links on every
  card, row, ticket and in the Past videos dialog, with a small two-platform
  mark on its cover. For Simplified Chinese readers a past video with a
  Bilibili copy opens on Bilibili first.
- YouTube titles the creator localized appear as translations, marked as the
  platform's own.
- The switch after a translated card title reads "AI"; its tooltip and
  accessible name still spell out the origin. The duplicate Past videos
  button under a shortened video grid is gone; the header button opens the
  same archive.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Operator console</strong></summary>

- The Sources page has a section per source with its timer, interval, read
  mode (X and YouTube), **Run now**, next run and last run. Run now runs the
  source's chain once and leaves the next timed run as planned. The YouTube
  section also holds push notifications and the live-status probe.
  `GET /api/admin/sources` lists the timers as `schedules`.
- The workflow list and editor are gone. The Runs page keeps the run history
  and offers a one-off step run instead: pick steps from
  `GET /api/admin/runs/steps` and run them once with
  `{"kind": "workflow", "steps": [...]}`. A failed run of any kind can be
  retried when a step failed temporarily. Run history labels source runs by
  source ("X timer", "X · run now") and older saved-workflow runs as retired
  workflows, and run listings load in a fixed number of queries.
- YouTube fetches offer a `count` window read from the uploads listing.
  Without a key it reaches only the RSS feed's 15 entries and reports
  `youtube_count_limited` above that.
- Fetching lives on the Sources page only. The Posts and Videos pages open
  straight on their lists, with a Fetch button in the header that leads
  there, and adding a Bilibili upload by BV ID moves to the Bilibili section
  of the Sources page.
- Posts whose extraction keeps failing show when they are retried next, or
  that automatic retries stopped, on the Posts and Review pages.
  `GET /api/admin/posts` adds `llmRetryAt` and `llmRetryStopped`.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Synchronization and extraction</strong></summary>

- Each source runs on its own timer: X (default every 30 minutes), YouTube
  (60) and Bilibili (180, never below `BILIBILI_MIN_INTERVAL_MINUTES`) each
  submit a fixed chain through the same single-flight queue:
  `x, media, schedule, translate`, `youtube, media, translate` and
  `bilibili, media, translate`, followed by `reconcile`. The scheduler starts
  whichever due timer was planned earliest, and a fresh installation keeps
  the timers off. YouTube pushes queue the `youtube` chain, so pushed videos
  are translated too.
- Each planned run moves by a random offset of up to ±10% of its interval;
  Bilibili only runs later, never sooner than
  `BILIBILI_MIN_INTERVAL_MINUTES`. Retrying a timed run, or recovering it
  after an expired lease, keeps the timer's planned next run.
- X and YouTube timers read by count instead of by days. Each source is
  `incremental` (the default: newest first until a stored record, at most
  `max` new ones, 50 by default) or `count` (the newest N every run), each
  1–200. Run now, one-off step runs and YouTube pushes read the same way.
- Credentials no longer turn a scheduled run into a date search. X timers
  always use the FxTwitter incremental path, keeping `X_API_BEARER_TOKEN` for
  `before` and `range` windows. With `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`, the recent step pages
  the uploads playlist (one quota unit per 50 uploads) instead of
  `search.list`.
- An incremental X run that hits its cap keeps a continuation and reads the
  rest on later runs instead of skipping posts; a newer gap keeps an older
  one's progress, and a pinned post does not close a gap early. A timeline
  that ends first reports `x_timeline_gap`. An incremental YouTube run keeps
  its cursor while new uploads above it are unread, so later runs take them.
- Incremental X runs store a status straight from the timeline page they
  already read and keep vxtwitter detail requests for statuses listed
  without content, pending statuses no longer listed, known-post refreshes
  and profile fallbacks. `X_MAX_STATUS_REQUESTS` now bounds only those
  requests, so a backlog drains in fewer runs; the step result counts
  `timelinePosts`.
- Pending X statuses stop being requested forever: two consecutive 404/410
  (or other-account) answers drop one with `x_status_gone`, and other
  failures wait 1, 6 and 24 hours before the fourth drops it with
  `x_retry_limit`. The step result reports `pendingHeld` and
  `pendingDropped`.
- Schedule extraction backs off posts that keep failing: 1, 6 and 24 hours
  after the first three consecutive failures of the same input, and no
  automatic retry after the fourth. New content, a changed model queue or an
  operator reprocess starts it over. Held posts make no model call and are
  counted as `backoff` or `retryStopped`.
- With `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`, the live-status probe asks one `videos.list` call
  about all of a run's due videos before reading any watch page (one quota
  unit per run; the run result counts `apiRequests`), and titles the creator
  localized on YouTube are stored as `youtube` platform translations (`en`,
  and `zh-CN` from `zh`, `zh-CN` or `zh-Hans`). Manual translations still
  win, and a video with both locales covered is not sent to the translation
  model.

</details>
